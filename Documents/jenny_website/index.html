Top Places to Explore


Buddy’s Restaurant -
📍Lucban, Quezon

Buddy’s Restaurant is known for its hearty Filipino comfort food, featuring flavorful and colorful dishes inspired by the vibrant cuisine of Lucban, Quezon. Famous for its Pancit Lucban (pancit habhab), Buddy’s also serves deliciously grilled meats, rich stews, and local specialties, all served in a festive, homey atmosphere that feels like a celebration with every meal.


Gumbo Restaurant -
📍Mandaluyong city

Gumbo Restaurant serves up rich, soulful flavors inspired by the heart of Louisiana cuisine. Known for its signature gumbo—a hearty stew of tender meats, fresh seafood, and aromatic spices—every dish bursts with Cajun and Creole flair. From jambalaya to seafood platters, Gumbo Restaurant offers a warm, flavorful dining experience that feels like a taste of New Orleans in every bite.


Marugame Restaurant -
📍Quezon city

Marugame Restaurant specializes in freshly made, handcrafted Japanese udon noodles and tempura, served in a casual, open-kitchen style. Guests can watch the udon-making process, ensuring each bowl is perfectly chewy and flavorful, paired with savory broths and customizable toppings. With crisp, golden tempura and a variety of side dishes, Marugame offers an authentic, comforting taste of Japan in every meal.


Ramen Nagi -
📍Makati city

Ramen Nagi is famous for its bold, flavorful bowls of Japanese ramen, crafted with rich, slow-cooked broths and perfectly springy noodles. Each bowl is customizable, from the richness of the soup to the spice level, and features premium toppings like tender chashu pork, marinated eggs, and fresh vegetables. Known for its creative ramen varieties, Ramen Nagi delivers a hearty, satisfying experience that blends authentic tradition with exciting flavors.


Shakey’s Restaurant -
📍Bangued, Abra

Shakey’s Restaurant is a beloved spot for classic, crowd-pleasing comfort food, best known for its thin-crust pizzas loaded with flavorful toppings, golden fried chicken, and crispy Mojo potatoes. With its fun, family-friendly vibe and a menu perfect for sharing, Shakey’s serves up a delicious mix of savory, satisfying favorites that bring people together over every meal.


Pizza Hut -
📍Pasay City

Pizza Hut is a global favorite for its hot, cheesy pizzas topped with generous portions of fresh ingredients and served on signature crusts, from classic pan to stuffed and thin. Alongside its famous pizzas, the menu features flavorful pastas, wings, and sides, making it a go-to spot for hearty, satisfying meals perfect for sharing with family and friends.
